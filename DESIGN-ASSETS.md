# Pantry design assets

The homepage, grain and spice editorial images were generated for the selected option-3 redesign using the built-in image-generation tool. They illustrate pantry ingredients and are not photographs of Oringo's warehouse or exact product packs. No branded packaging was generated for the implementation.

Prompts specified warm natural kitchen-table photography, fresh yam, white rice, unbranded red palm oil and cooking spices; no text, logos or invented branded packs. The grain banner shows rice, yam and cereals; the spice banner shows curry, jollof seasoning and thyme in wooden bowls.

Production assets are `images/design/pantry-hero.webp`, `images/design/grains-category.webp` and `images/design/spices-category.webp`, converted from generated source PNGs to WebP for transfer size. Existing catalogue product images are unchanged.

Fonts are self-hosted Inter and Barlow Condensed from Google Fonts, compressed to WOFF without altering outlines. License notices are in `fonts/OFL-Inter.txt` and `fonts/OFL-Barlow.txt`.
