Who We Are

Oringo Distributions is a dedicated importer and distributor of authentic ethnic food and beverages. We specialize in sourcing high-quality goods that celebrate cultural heritage, with a focus on bringing the rich flavors and traditions of Nigeria (and beyond) to homes, retailers, and restaurants.

From traditional staples like Garri, Elubo, and Palm Oil to specialty spices and snacks, our mission is to connect communities with the tastes they love, while ensuring quality, reliability, and excellent service every step of the way.

With a deep commitment to cultural heritage and customer satisfaction, we proudly serve as a vital link between home and diaspora communities. We are dedicated to bringing the rich flavours, traditions, and heritage of Nigeria and West Africa to homes, communities, retailers, and restaurants across the UK.

About Oringo Distributions FAQs

What does Oringo Distributions specialise in?

Oringo Distributions is a Nigerian grocery and beverage supplier based in Smethwick, Birmingham. We serve households and welcome wholesale enquiries for food staples, cooking ingredients, seasonings, non-alcoholic drinks and selected everyday essentials. Explore our current product range.

Is Oringo a UK-based business or do I order directly from Nigeria?

Oringo Distributions operates from Fast Safe Storage, Cornwall Road, Smethwick, Birmingham B66 2JR, United Kingdom. Customers arrange purchases with our UK business for local shopping, collection or quoted delivery to the UK and Europe.

Does Oringo serve customers outside Birmingham?

Yes. Customers outside Birmingham can enquire about courier orders across the UK and EU. Oringo confirms service availability for the destination and products before accepting the order. Our physical shopping and collection location is in Smethwick.

Can restaurants, caterers and shops request Nigerian grocery supplies?

Yes. Oringo welcomes wholesale enquiries from food businesses, caterers and shops as well as retail customers. Send your product list, brands, pack sizes, quantities and delivery postcode for a quotation. Availability and commercial terms are confirmed for each order.

Can I ask about groceries for a party or community event?

Yes. Send Oringo your food and drink list, quantities, event date and collection or delivery preference. We can check the requested products and discuss an order. Please confirm availability and timing with us before relying on an order for your event.

How can I contact the Oringo team directly?

Use WhatsApp +44 7534 116006, call +44 7534 116006 or email oringodistributions@gmail.com. Our contact page has the address, opening hours and information for planning a visit.
