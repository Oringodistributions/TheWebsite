ABOUT ORINGO

Helping businesses stock the flavours customers love.

Oringo Distributions brings together Nigerian groceries, familiar beverages and practical everyday essentials for wholesale buyers. Our range is held in a UK warehouse, with enquiries welcome from businesses across the UK, Republic of Ireland and Europe.

Explore our range and speak directly with Oringo about quantities, pack details and privately negotiated wholesale prices. Our catalogue includes groceries, drinks, traditional cooking ingredients, personal care products and kitchen essentials.

We welcome wholesale enquiries from the United Kingdom, Republic of Ireland, Western Europe and Eastern Europe.
