## Warehouse photography instructions
- Take **one full front-facing picture per SKU/flavour/format**; do not shoot a pile and expect it to match all products.
- Use original rear iPhone/main-phone camera at **1x**, in ordinary Photo mode with a cleaned lens, level and sharply focused on the product lettering.
- Use daylight / soft diffused light; no harsh flash or glossy glare. Place one product on a clean white background, upright with whole label and bottom weight visible. No hands, shelf price labels or neighbouring goods.
- A second back/side picture can confirm pack size, manufacturer label, barcode and nutritional text when unclear. For ingredients, sealed labelled packet is the **primary hero image**, bowl-of-food scene optional as secondary.
- Send 10–15 original files per batch, labelled using `SKU_product_brand_weight_format.jpg` when possible. Avoid screenshots or overly compressed messaging-app copies.
- Preferred final display image: square 1600×1600 px with real packaging visible and extra clear space. Do not claim an upscaled/repainted label is a real photograph.
- Process only after exact variant matching; remove background without redrawing packaging or misrepresenting a brand.
- For publishing: owner confirms brand, size, image accuracy, rights to use, quality and SKU before `photo_approved` may change to true. Existing draft photo badges should remain while pending.
