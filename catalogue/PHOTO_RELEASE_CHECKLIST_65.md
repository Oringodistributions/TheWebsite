## Image release steps for EACH SKU
1. Confirm one active product and the actual variant/size (carton quantity is separate from the unit pack).
2. Use Oringo's own image, manufacturer-authorised media, or documented commercial-use licence.
3. Inspect labels, cap colour, package shape and regional design for exact match.
4. Prefer front-facing photo, entire product visible, neutral bright lighting and plain background.
5. Crop cleanly into a square **without inventing hidden label elements** or digitally changing package claims.
6. Add descriptive image alt text tied to the exact product and size.
7. Record client's explicit approval of photo and published listing.
8. Only then set `photo_approved: true`, `photo_status: "APPROVED"`, and add the authorised local `image_url` to the active catalogue JSON.
