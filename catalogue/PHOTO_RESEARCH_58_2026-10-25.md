# Oringo: 58 missing catalogue photos — online sourcing audit

Research date: 25 October 2026. Inventory basis: 76 confirmed in-stock product records on `main`; 18 have linked photos; 58 have no linked photos.

**Important:** The existence of a manufacturer page or retail photograph is NOT a commercial image licence. None of these 58 images is approved for publication by this audit. Confirm actual stock packaging and obtain rights before linking an image.

## Results
- 19 SKUs have verified official manufacturer/brand reference pages that can be used to identify the product family and request an authorised photo. These are NOT 19 exact matched/approved photos.
- 39 SKUs still require Oringo warehouse photos or additional licensed assets. This includes 20 entries with no original image, 18 other third-party reference-only entries and one Garri shelf crop.
- One notable catalogue discrepancy: Peak Milk Powder Pouch is listed at 400g but the manufacturer's online assortment shows 350g and 800g pouches; verify stock before publication.

## Warehouse photo standard
1. Take one front-facing image per exact pack size/flavour against a clean background, in daylight without glare.
2. Take a separate back/side label image for food ingredients, allergens, weight and storage details, or for dimensions on utensils.
3. State product name, size, flavour, SKU and whether the photo was taken by Oringo or supplied with written marketing permission.
4. Send photos in batches of 10–15; preserve original package artwork and do not generate synthetic product labels.
5. Before linking a third-party photo to the live site, confirm its source, exact product match, commercial permission and visual quality.

## Other cautions
- Multi-format entries ORI-016, ORI-017, ORI-018, ORI-020, ORI-021, ORI-022 need splitting or explicit variant selection; one mixed photo must not represent a single exact SKU.
- Regulatory/compliance work on food trading and European shipping remains separate from photo sourcing.
