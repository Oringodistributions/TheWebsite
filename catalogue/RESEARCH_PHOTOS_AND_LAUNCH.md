## Copyright and quality gate
UK IPO guidance: Images found online **generally need permission for commercial website use**. [Official guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/copyright-notice-digital-images-photographs-and-the-internet/copyright-notice-digital-images-photographs-and-the-internet).
- Source product **identification/reference** photos for internal review only. Do not hotlink competitor pages or copy retailer photos into live product cards without rights.
- Prefer (1) Oringo's own true front-facing photographs, then (2) manufacturer-authorised media or an explicit supplier's commercial licence.
- Package text, cap colour, pack size and regional formula must match the actual stock.
- Publish a product image only after written licence/ownership, pack-variant match, crop clarity and client approval.
- Record source URL, rights holder, permission terms and date for each photo.

## Compliance review before launch
- **Alcohol:** September 2026 UK licensing guidance for remote sales explains that the premises where alcohol is appropriated to an online order needs suitable authorisation and delivery age-verification requirements apply. A website "18+" click is **not sufficient**. [GOV.UK source](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/explanatory-memorandum-revised-guidance-issued-under-s-182-of-licensing-act-2003/revised-guidance-issued-under-section-182-of-the-licensing-act-2003-september-2026-accessible-version). Owner to confirm any relevant alcohol licence before activating adult-beverage enquiries/ordering.
- **Robb Ointment:** Confirm the applicable MHRA marketing authorisation/import status before advertising medicinal benefits or offering online sale in the UK; don't infer permission from stock possession. [MHRA guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/advertise-your-medicines), [import rules](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/import-a-human-medicine).
- **Europe delivery:** Validate product/category import and shipping rules for each country and never promise blanket coverage, free delivery or timelines without agreement.
- **EU/UK customer data:** A WhatsApp enquiry initiates contact; do not add unsolicited marketing opt-ins or third-party analytics without appropriate disclosures.
- **Website:** Keep draft PR open and unmerged, confirm images/rights/legal requirements, then manually test iPhone and desktop and owner-approve before live deployment.
