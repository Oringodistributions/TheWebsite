Visits, Delivery & Payment FAQs

Where is Oringo Distributions in Smethwick, Birmingham?

Find Oringo at Fast Safe Storage, Cornwall Road, Smethwick, Birmingham B66 2JR, United Kingdom. Customers can visit and shop or arrange collection. Use the address and directions on this page when planning your journey.

What are Oringo’s opening hours, and is parking free?

Oringo is open 24 hours Monday to Saturday and closed on Sundays, using UK local time. Free parking is available for visitors. Contact the team if you need help planning your visit or collecting a particular order.

How do I arrange grocery collection from Oringo?

Send your selected products and quantities to WhatsApp +44 7534 116006 and tell us you want to collect. Agree the order, price and collection arrangements with our team before travelling for that order.

What details are needed for a Birmingham or UK delivery quote?

Send your product names, pack sizes, quantities and delivery postcode. For nearby delivery, include your area around Smethwick or Birmingham. Oringo will confirm whether local delivery or a courier is suitable and quote the cost and estimated timing before you agree to buy.

How much is delivery, and do you offer next-day delivery?

Delivery charges and estimated times are confirmed individually for the products, order size and destination. This website does not promise a flat delivery fee or next-day arrival. Tell us your required date so we can check available options before you confirm your order.

What should I send for a European courier delivery enquiry?

Include your destination country, postcode, full product list and quantities. Oringo checks the requested goods against destination-specific restrictions and available courier services. The final quote includes delivery and applicable customs duties and import taxes before you agree to purchase.

Which payment methods can I use at Oringo?

Oringo accepts cash, debit and credit cards, contactless payments and bank transfers. Confirm the suitable payment arrangement with the team for your visit or WhatsApp order. There is no card checkout on this website; do not send full card details in a message.

Has my enquiry been sent when I use the contact form?

The contact form opens a draft in your own email app. Review and send that email to complete the enquiry. If the email app does not open, email oringodistributions@gmail.com directly or contact us on WhatsApp.
