    Product & Ordering FAQs

    Can I buy local stoneless rice and poundo yam from Oringo?

    Yes. Local Stoneless Rice, Ayoola Poundo Yam Flour and Mealmate Poundo Yam Flour are listed in our catalogue. Search by product or brand, then ask us to confirm the available weight, pack size and price before ordering.

    Which ingredients can I find for Nigerian jollof rice?

    Our catalogue includes local stoneless rice, Sonia Tomato Mix, Erisco Tomato Paste, AACE Foods Jollof Rice Seasoning, Larsor Jollof Seasoning, Friel Thyme and cooking oils. Search the catalogue for each ingredient and confirm the quantities you need with Oringo.

    Does Oringo stock Nigerian malt drinks and milk powders?

    Our photographed range includes Beta Malt, Grand Malt, Viju Wheat Flavoured Milk Drink, Peak Full Cream Milk Powder Tin and Dano Cool Cow Instant Filled Milk Powder. Milo, Bournvita and Ovaltine are also listed. Ask us about the exact pack or quantity required.

    Does Buy Now take payment or confirm my order?

    No. Buy Now opens a prefilled WhatsApp enquiry for the selected product. You still need to send the message and agree your order with Oringo. The enquiry basket lets you combine products in one message; this website has no card-payment checkout.

    Why are product prices and delivery charges quoted on WhatsApp?

    Oringo provides a quotation for your chosen products, pack sizes, quantities and destination. You can review the product prices, delivery charge and final total before agreeing to buy. Contact us for the current price rather than relying on prices printed in a product photograph.

    How do I check ingredients, allergens or a particular pack size?

    Ask Oringo for the information on the exact product and pack you intend to buy before confirming your order. A front-of-pack photograph may not show the full ingredient, allergen, storage or date information. Do not assume different sizes or flavours have identical ingredients.

    Can I request a product that is missing from the online catalogue?

    Yes. Send the product name, brand, size and, if possible, a photograph to WhatsApp +44 7534 116006. We will check availability. A request does not mean an unlisted product is in stock, and the website only displays listings with matching product images.
