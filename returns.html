Returns, Refunds & Cancellation Policy

Oringo Distributions supplies Nigerian groceries, non-alcoholic beverages and household essentials through enquiries and orders arranged on WhatsApp. This policy explains our approach to returns, refunds and cancellations for retail customers. Mandatory consumer rights always take priority.

Before placing an order: We will confirm the selected goods, quantities, prices, any delivery charges and delivery arrangements. A WhatsApp enquiry is not itself a completed purchase.

1. Cancelling an order

You can withdraw an enquiry before entering a binding purchase agreement. For eligible UK consumer distance purchases, you generally have 14 days from receiving the goods to tell us you wish to cancel, followed by another 14 days to return them. You can notify us by email or WhatsApp with a clear statement that you wish to cancel.

Some goods are exempt from change-of-mind cancellation rights, including goods liable to deteriorate or expire rapidly and certain sealed goods unsuitable for return for health or hygiene reasons once unsealed. Ordinary non-perishable packaged groceries are not automatically exempt. Your statutory rights concerning faulty or misdescribed goods are unaffected.

3. Who pays return postage?

  • Change-of-mind returns: You pay the direct return postage costs, where we informed you of this responsibility before the order was placed.
  • Faulty, damaged, misdescribed or incorrectly supplied products: Oringo covers reasonable return costs where required by applicable law.

Goods should only be handled as necessary to inspect them. A lawful deduction may apply if excessive handling has reduced their value.

4. Faulty, damaged or incorrect products

Contact us as soon as reasonably possible if an item is faulty, unsafe, damaged, incorrect or not as described. Please provide your order details and any helpful photographs, if available. Photographs are helpful but are not an absolute condition of your legal rights.

For qualifying UK consumer purchases, the short-term right to reject faulty goods is generally 30 days, although goods reasonably expected to perish sooner may have a shorter period. Other legal remedies may apply after that period. We will provide the remedy required by law.

5. Refunds

Where a statutory change-of-mind cancellation is valid, we will refund eligible payments, including the standard outbound delivery charge where required by law. Upgraded delivery costs beyond standard delivery need not be refunded. We may wait until goods are received or evidence of their return is supplied before issuing the refund, as permitted by law.

Refunds for returned goods will be made without undue delay and no later than 14 days after receiving the goods back or evidence that they were sent back, whichever is earlier. Other applicable statutory refund deadlines remain in force. Refunds normally use the original payment method unless you expressly agree otherwise, without a refund fee.

6. Delivery problems

If your order is late, missing, incomplete or damaged in transit, please contact us. We will investigate and comply with your statutory rights, including applicable rights where an agreed delivery deadline is missed.

7. Orders outside the UK

We welcome orders from customers across Europe. Before accepting payment or confirming an international order, we will check destination-specific food/import restrictions, whether the requested products can be shipped, delivery availability, charges and estimated timing. Oringo will include applicable customs duties, import taxes and delivery charges in the final price quoted to European customers before they agree to purchase. We will check the destination-specific requirements when preparing each quotation, rather than charging an unspecified amount after the order is placed. Mandatory consumer protections in the destination country may apply. Please contact us before arranging an international return for instructions. This policy does not exclude those protections.

8. Wholesale purchases

Business-to-business orders may be governed by separately agreed commercial terms. UK consumer-only change-of-mind rights do not automatically apply to wholesale purchases made by businesses.

9. Food safety and allergen information

Check ingredient, allergen, storage and date information before consuming products. Contact us before ordering if you need product-specific information. Required allergen information will be made available before purchase and at delivery as applicable.

Returns & Order Support FAQs

What should I do if my Oringo grocery order is damaged, wrong or incomplete?

Contact Oringo as soon as reasonably possible with your order details and a description of the issue. Photographs are helpful if available, but are not a condition of your legal rights. We will investigate and provide the remedy required by applicable law.

Can I cancel a WhatsApp enquiry before agreeing to buy?

Yes. An enquiry or a prefilled WhatsApp message is not itself a completed purchase. You can withdraw the enquiry before entering a binding purchase agreement. For an agreed purchase, contact us with a clear cancellation request; the policy above explains eligibility and exceptions.

Can I return non-perishable groceries bought at a distance?

For eligible UK consumer distance purchases, you generally have 14 days after receipt to tell us you wish to cancel, then another 14 days to return the goods. Ordinary non-perishable packaged groceries are not automatically excluded. See the cancellation section above for exceptions and conditions.

Are perishable foods or opened hygiene products eligible for change-of-mind returns?

Goods that deteriorate or expire rapidly, and certain sealed health or hygiene goods once unsealed, may be exempt from change-of-mind cancellation. This does not remove your rights if goods are faulty, incorrect or not as described. Contact us about the particular item.

Who pays postage when I return an order?

For eligible change-of-mind returns, you pay direct return postage where this responsibility was explained before purchase. Oringo covers reasonable return costs for faulty, damaged, misdescribed or incorrectly supplied goods where required by law. Contact us for practical return instructions.

How do I ask about a refund or a missing delivery?

Email oringodistributions@gmail.com or use WhatsApp +44 7534 116006 with your name and order details. The policy above explains refund timing and payment methods. We will investigate late, missing or damaged deliveries; please contact us before arranging a return from outside the UK.
